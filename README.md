### About

Systems Engineering student finishing my degree at Universidad del Valle (Cali, Colombia), with professional experience since 2023 across frontend, backend, and applied NLP.

My undergraduate thesis builds a named entity recognition system for Spanish clinical text, comparing CRF, BiLSTM, RoBERTa, and LLM approaches. In 2026 I completed a research internship at the **PReCISE group**, Université de Namur (Belgium), working on variability modeling and formal methods.
